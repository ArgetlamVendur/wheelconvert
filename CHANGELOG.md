### Added
- First release candidate: tray app with a Shift-drag or hotkey wheel.
- Image conversion (JPG, PNG, WebP, ICO from common formats).
- Audio and video conversion through an external FFmpeg (not bundled).
- PDF tools: split, halves, odd/even, rotate, reverse, reorder, pages to images.
- Per-user installer (no administrator rights) and a portable zip, with SHA-256 checksums.
